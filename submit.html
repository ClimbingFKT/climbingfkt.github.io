Submit an FKT

Have a Colorado climbing FKT to submit? Send us an email with the details below. All submissions are reviewed before publication.

Submission Details

  • Route name and location
  • Athlete name(s)
  • Date and elapsed time (HH:MM:SS)
  • Climbing style (solo, roped, simul-climbing, etc.)
  • Unsupported, Self-supported or Supported. Fixed lines are always considered supported
  • Start and finish points
  • Verification (Strava activity, GPS track, etc.)
  • Trip report and splits (optional)

Verification & Permission

Records must be supported by verifiable primary-source evidence, such as a gps file. Athlete permission is required before publishing personal information or submitted materials.

Submit by Email

Email your submission to contact@climbingfkt.com
