Climbing Safety

Rock climbing, alpine climbing and related mountain activities are inherently dangerous and can result in serious injury or death. Climbers are responsible for assessing their abilities, conditions, risks and appropriate safety equipment. This website documents climbing speed records for historical and informational purposes only. Published speed records are not recommendations to attempt these routes or times.
